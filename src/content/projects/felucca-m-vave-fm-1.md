M-Vave opened the firmware of its cheap FM-1 sampler in October 2026, and Leo Kuroshita's Felucca is the community firmware that grew around it, the most starred of the 2026 custom-firmware wave. What sets it apart for this log is the README: a plain statement of what AI agents did (code, tests, docs, translation, maintenance) and what they did not (design, sounds, artwork).
