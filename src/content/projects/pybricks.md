LEGO discontinued the MINDSTORMS Robot Inventor app on October 1, 2026, with no further updates. Pybricks replaces the hub's firmware with MicroPython, installed from the browser at code.pybricks.com, and programs are written and run from the same page. The robot keeps working with no LEGO app at all.
