Korg sold two Electribes with the same hardware and different firmware, then stopped updating both. bangcorrupt's Hacktribe builds on the sampler firmware and runs on both models, so the synth gets sampling, plus new filters, oscillators, effects, grooves, scales and a fuller MIDI implementation. The project is now archived, but the releases and the write-ups stay up.
