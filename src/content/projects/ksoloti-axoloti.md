Axoloti was a board for sketching DSP patches that run on their own hardware. Its creator stopped around 2019 and the patcher stopped getting updates. Ksoloti is a community fork of the patcher and firmware that keeps the original Axoloti Core boards working, alongside the team's own replacement board.
