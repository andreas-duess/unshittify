Elektron stopped updating the Model:Cycles in 2021. 18nelli's browser flasher takes the owner's own OS files and builds a modded one: a sampler machine (TinyGregAudio's Model-TG), Syntakt engines, a Braids-based macro machine, a 303-style acid machine, an arpeggiator, extra LFOs and six-track USB audio. It is reversible.
