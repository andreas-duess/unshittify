Deep88 wanted a track mute page on the MPC3000 for about twenty years. Akai's last OS is 3.12, so he built one himself on top of it: track mute, note repeat, sampling while the sequencer runs, incremental chop, bit crush, normalize, mute groups and a setup menu. It boots from a floppy or a Gotek drive and leaves the stock OS alone. Free, as is, "in the spirit of the old internet".
